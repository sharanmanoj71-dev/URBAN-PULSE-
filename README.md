# UrbanPulse – Smart Civic Issue Reporting and City Intelligence Platform

**Tagline: Your City. Your Voice. Real Action.**

## Project Description

UrbanPulse is a smart civic issue reporting and city intelligence platform designed to connect citizens with local authorities and help create cleaner, safer, and better-managed communities.

The platform allows citizens to report everyday civic problems such as damaged roads, potholes, water leakage, overflowing garbage, drainage issues, broken streetlights, and other public infrastructure problems. Users can submit complaints with descriptions, images, and location details, making it easier to identify and understand issues in their surroundings.

UrbanPulse provides an interactive dashboard and live map to visualize reported issues geographically. Complaints are organized into different categories and tracked through statuses such as Pending, In Progress, and Resolved. This helps citizens monitor the progress of their reports while giving administrators a centralized view of community concerns.

The platform also aims to use AI-assisted analysis to categorize complaints, identify recurring problems, and support better decision-making and prioritization. Administrators can monitor reports, review complaint details, update statuses, and understand patterns through dashboard insights.

## Key Features

1. **Citizen Issue Reporting:** Submit civic complaints with descriptions, photographs, and location information.
2. **Interactive Live Map:** View reported issues on a geographical map.
3. **Multiple Complaint Categories:** Organize issues into Roads & Transit, Utilities & Public Safety, Water & Sanitation, and Public Spaces & Environment.
4. **Complaint Tracking:** Monitor reports through Pending, In Progress, and Resolved statuses.
5. **Admin Dashboard:** Manage complaints and monitor civic issue statistics.
6. **AI-Assisted Analysis:** Support complaint classification and analysis of reported issues.
7. **Location-Based Reporting:** Help identify the geographical areas affected by civic problems.
8. **Authentication:** Provide a foundation for secure user access through Firebase Authentication.
9. **Backend Integration:** Use Supabase for database and storage capabilities.

## Project Objective

The primary objective of UrbanPulse is to improve communication between citizens and local authorities by making civic issue reporting more accessible, transparent, and organized. It aims to reduce the gap between identifying a public problem and taking action to resolve it.

## Target Area

The initial demonstration focuses on Mijar and nearby areas of Mangaluru, Karnataka, using illustrative synthetic complaint records to demonstrate the platform's reporting, mapping, and dashboard capabilities.

## Expected Outcome

UrbanPulse aims to transform traditional civic complaint processes into a more transparent, technology-driven system where citizens can raise concerns, authorities can manage them efficiently, and communities can work together toward better urban infrastructure and public services.
